
Leave Policy. Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual leave per year.
Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over to the next year.
Sick leave: 10 days per year, a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 days.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks; paternity leave is 2 weeks.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.
