MARINER SHIPPING LTD.
LEAVE POLICY
Applicable to: Shore Staff
Document ID: HR-POL-001
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This policy establishes the rules and procedures governing employee leave at Mariner Shipping Ltd. It is designed to support employee well-being while ensuring continuity of business operations.

1.2 This policy applies to all permanent and fixed-term shore-based employees working at the company's offices, operational centres, and administrative locations.

1.3 Employees assigned to vessels or offshore operations are governed by their respective employment agreements and operational leave arrangements.

1.4 All leave must be requested through the HR portal and approved before the employee begins their absence, except in emergencies.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE

2.1 Eligible full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave for each completed calendar year.

2.2 Employees joining during the calendar year receive annual leave on a prorated basis, calculated at 2 working days for each completed month of service.

2.3 Annual leave should be requested at least 7 calendar days before the intended start date. Requests involving more than 10 consecutive working days should be submitted at least 21 calendar days in advance.

2.4 Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March. Any remaining balance after this date will normally expire.

2.5 Exceptions to the carry-forward limit may be approved by the HR Director where business requirements, medical circumstances, or management-directed leave restrictions prevented the employee from taking leave.

3. PROBATION AND ELIGIBILITY

3.1 Employees serving a probationary period of 6 months continue to accrue annual leave at the normal rate.

3.2 During probation, annual leave should normally be limited to 5 working days at a time and requires approval from the employee's Department Head and HR Manager.

3.3 Emergency medical leave and statutory leave are not restricted by the probationary annual leave limitation.

3.4 Employees who resign or are terminated will have their outstanding leave balance calculated up to their final working day, subject to applicable employment terms and local law.

4. SICK LEAVE

4.1 Eligible employees receive 10 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

4.2 Employees must notify their line manager within 2 hours of their scheduled starting time when unable to attend work due to illness.

4.3 A medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner is required for an absence exceeding 2 consecutive working days.

4.4 In cases of extended illness, HR may request additional medical documentation where permitted by law.

4.5 Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward or converted into cash.

5. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE

5.1 Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of maternity leave, subject to applicable local employment laws and eligibility requirements.

5.2 Employees should notify HR at least 30 calendar days before the expected commencement of maternity leave wherever reasonably possible.

5.3 Eligible employees receive 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, normally taken within 3 months following the birth or adoption of a child.

5.4 Additional leave arrangements may be considered by HR in accordance with applicable law.

6. PEAK-SEASON RESTRICTIONS

6.1 To maintain operational continuity, annual leave may be restricted during designated peak shipping periods.

6.2 The standard peak-season blackout period is 1 November to 15 December each year.

6.3 Leave during this period requires exceptional approval from the Chief Operating Officer and HR Director.

6.4 Emergency medical leave, maternity leave, statutory leave, and other legally protected absences are not prohibited by this restriction.

7. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

7.1 Leave approval follows the employee's seniority and reporting structure.

7.2 Requests of up to 3 consecutive working days require approval from the employee's Line Manager.

7.3 Requests of 4 to 10 consecutive working days require approval from the Department Head.

7.4 Requests exceeding 10 consecutive working days require approval from the Department Head and HR Manager.

7.5 Exceptions involving peak-season restrictions or carry-forward limits require approval from the HR Director and, where specified, the Chief Operating Officer.

7.6 Senior management leave requests require approval from the Chief Executive Officer or the designated Board representative.

8. TRAVEL DURING LEAVE

8.1 Employees travelling for personal purposes during approved leave are responsible for their own travel expenses.

8.2 Business travel immediately before, during, or after an approved leave period must be documented and approved separately.

8.3 Employees must refer to Section 3.2 of the Travel & Expense Policy for rules governing business travel combined with personal leave.

9. CONDUCT AND RESPONSIBILITIES

9.1 Employees must provide accurate information when submitting leave requests.

9.2 Employees must not misrepresent medical circumstances or submit fraudulent supporting documents.

9.3 Misuse of leave may result in disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct, Section 5.

10. POLICY ADMINISTRATION

10.1 HR is responsible for maintaining employee leave records and monitoring compliance.

10.2 Department Heads must ensure adequate operational coverage before approving leave.

10.3 This policy will be reviewed annually or when operational or legal requirements change.

10.4 Questions concerning interpretation should be directed to the HR Department.
