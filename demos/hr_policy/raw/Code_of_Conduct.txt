MARINER SHIPPING LTD.
CODE OF CONDUCT
Applicable to: Shore Staff
Document ID: GOV-POL-003
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

1.1 This Code of Conduct defines the professional and ethical standards expected of all employees of Mariner Shipping Ltd.

1.2 It applies to permanent employees, fixed-term employees, managers, contractors, and other authorised personnel working in shore-based operations.

1.3 Employees are expected to act honestly, responsibly, respectfully, and in the best interests of the company.

2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOUR

2.1 Employees must perform their responsibilities with integrity, diligence, and professional competence.

2.2 Employees must treat colleagues, suppliers, customers, port authorities, and business partners with courtesy and respect.

2.3 Discrimination, bullying, intimidation, retaliation, and harassment are prohibited.

2.4 Employees must not falsify operational records, financial documents, attendance information, expense claims, or business communications.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INFORMATION SECURITY

3.1 Employees must protect confidential company information, supplier agreements, customer records, pricing information, and operational data.

3.2 Confidential information must only be shared with authorised individuals for legitimate business purposes.

3.3 Company passwords, access credentials, and restricted documents must not be disclosed to unauthorised parties.

3.4 Suspected information security incidents must be reported to the IT Security Team within 24 hours of discovery.

4. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

4.1 A conflict of interest exists when an employee's personal, financial, or external interests may interfere with their professional responsibilities.

4.2 Employees must disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest to the Compliance Officer within 5 working days of becoming aware of them.

4.3 Employees must not participate in supplier selection, contract approval, recruitment, or procurement decisions where they have an undisclosed personal or financial interest.

4.4 Employees must not use company information or authority to obtain personal benefits.

4.5 The Compliance Officer must review reported conflicts within 10 working days and determine whether additional controls or reassignment of responsibilities are necessary.

5. GIFTS, HOSPITALITY AND BUSINESS ETHICS

5.1 Employees must not request or accept cash, cash equivalents, personal loans, or other improper benefits from suppliers, customers, or business partners.

5.2 Modest promotional gifts valued at USD 50 or less may be accepted where they are occasional, transparent, and do not influence business decisions.

5.3 Gifts valued above USD 50 must be declared to the Compliance Officer within 2 working days. Acceptance requires written approval from the Department Head and Compliance Officer.

5.4 Gifts valued above USD 150 are generally prohibited unless the Chief Compliance Officer grants an exceptional written waiver for a legitimate business reason.

5.5 Business hospitality exceeding USD 100 per person must receive prior approval from the Department Head.

5.6 Employees must disclose gifts and hospitality offered to public officials, port authorities, or government representatives regardless of monetary value. Such offers require prior written Compliance approval and must comply with applicable anti-bribery laws.

5.7 Employees must follow Section 9 of the Travel & Expense Policy when handling gifts and hospitality during company travel.

6. HARASSMENT AND WORKPLACE REPORTING

6.1 Mariner Shipping Ltd. maintains a zero-tolerance approach to harassment, sexual harassment, discrimination, and workplace intimidation.

6.2 Employees experiencing or witnessing inappropriate conduct should report the incident through the confidential Ethics & Respect Hotline or directly to the HR Director.

6.3 Reports should be submitted as soon as reasonably possible and preferably within 5 working days of the incident.

6.4 The HR Director must acknowledge a formal complaint within 2 working days.

6.5 A preliminary assessment should begin within 5 working days. Where appropriate, a formal investigation should normally be completed within 20 working days.

6.6 Employees may request confidential handling of their reports. Information will be shared only with individuals who need it for investigation and resolution.

6.7 Retaliation against employees who raise concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited.

7. USE OF COMPANY PROPERTY

7.1 Company equipment, communication systems, vehicles, and facilities must be used responsibly and primarily for authorised business purposes.

7.2 Employees must not misuse company funds, property, or access rights for personal gain.

7.3 Loss, theft, or damage involving company property must be reported to the relevant Department Head within 24 hours.

8. DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURE

8.1 Alleged misconduct will be assessed fairly, consistently, and in accordance with applicable employment law.

8.2 Disciplinary action will normally follow these steps:

a. Step 1: Informal counselling and clarification of expectations.
b. Step 2: Formal written warning.
c. Step 3: Final written warning.
d. Step 4: Suspension pending investigation where legally permitted.
e. Step 5: Termination of employment for serious or repeated misconduct.

8.3 Serious misconduct, including fraud, bribery, harassment, deliberate falsification of records, or major confidentiality breaches, may justify immediate formal investigation and stronger disciplinary action.

8.4 Employees will normally be given an opportunity to respond to allegations before a disciplinary decision is made.

9. LEAVE AND EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES

9.1 Employees must follow the procedures established in the Leave Policy when requesting annual, sick, maternity, or paternity leave.

9.2 False medical documents, deliberate leave misrepresentation, or unauthorised absence may constitute misconduct under Section 8.

10. REPORTING AND ADMINISTRATION

10.1 Ethical concerns may be reported to the Compliance Officer or through the confidential Ethics & Respect Hotline.

10.2 Reports will be handled objectively and without discrimination or retaliation.

10.3 The Compliance Department is responsible for maintaining records of reported violations and disciplinary outcomes.

10.4 This Code will be reviewed annually and communicated to all employees.

END OF DOCUMENT
