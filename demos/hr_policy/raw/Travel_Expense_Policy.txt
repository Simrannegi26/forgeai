MARINER SHIPPING LTD.
TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Applicable to: Shore Staff
Document ID: FIN-POL-002
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This policy establishes the requirements for business travel, accommodation, meals, transportation, and reimbursement of expenses incurred by shore staff.

1.2 It applies to employees travelling for approved business meetings, supplier visits, port operations, conferences, inspections, and other authorised company activities.

1.3 All business travel must have a valid business purpose and prior approval.

2. TRAVEL AUTHORISATION

2.1 Employees must submit a travel request through the company travel portal at least 7 calendar days before domestic travel and 14 calendar days before international travel.

2.2 The request must include the destination, business purpose, travel dates, estimated expenses, and relevant project or department details.

2.3 The Line Manager may approve travel costing up to USD 1,500.

2.4 Travel costing between USD 1,501 and USD 5,000 requires Department Head approval.

2.5 Travel exceeding USD 5,000 requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer.

2.6 Emergency operational travel may be authorised by the Chief Operating Officer, with supporting documentation submitted within 2 working days.

3. TRAVEL AND LEAVE COMBINATIONS

3.1 Employees may combine approved business travel with personal leave where operational requirements permit.

3.2 When business travel is combined with personal leave, the company will pay only the expenses directly attributable to the approved business portion. Additional personal accommodation, meals, transport, and travel changes must be paid by the employee.

3.3 Employees must obtain written approval from their Line Manager and HR before combining business travel with annual leave. See Section 8.3 of the Leave Policy for the related leave-record requirements.

3.4 Employees must not claim personal travel costs as business expenses.

4. PER-DIEM ALLOWANCES

4.1 Employees travelling on approved business may claim daily meal and incidental allowances according to the following destination tiers.

4.2 Tier 1 cities, including London, New York, Singapore, and Tokyo, carry a maximum per-diem of USD 85 per day.

4.3 Tier 2 cities, including Dubai, Mumbai, Rotterdam, and Hamburg, carry a maximum per-diem of USD 65 per day.

4.4 Tier 3 cities and other approved destinations carry a maximum per-diem of USD 45 per day.

4.5 Per-diem allowances cover meals and reasonable incidental expenses. They do not cover hotel accommodation, airfare, or business entertainment.

4.6 Where meals are provided by a conference, client, or hotel, the corresponding meal allowance must be reduced by 30% for each provided meal.

5. ACCOMMODATION

5.1 Employees must select safe, business-appropriate accommodation within the approved company limits.

5.2 Standard hotel limits per night, excluding applicable taxes, are:

a. Tier 1 cities: USD 250.
b. Tier 2 cities: USD 180.
c. Tier 3 cities: USD 120.

5.3 Accommodation exceeding these limits requires prior written approval from the Department Head.

5.4 In exceptional circumstances, such as major shipping events or limited accommodation availability, the Chief Operating Officer may authorise a higher limit.

6. AIR TRAVEL

6.1 Economy class is mandatory for flights with a scheduled duration of up to 6 hours.

6.2 Premium economy may be approved for flights exceeding 6 hours where the additional cost is commercially reasonable.

6.3 Business class may be authorised for flights exceeding 10 hours for Department Heads and senior executives, subject to approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

6.4 First-class travel is not permitted unless specifically authorised by the Chief Executive Officer for exceptional business circumstances.

6.5 Employees must select reasonable fares and avoid unnecessary last-minute bookings.

7. RECEIPTS AND REIMBURSEMENTS

7.1 Original receipts or verifiable electronic receipts are required for individual expenses exceeding USD 25.

7.2 Expenses of USD 25 or less may be claimed without an individual receipt if supported by a valid expense declaration.

7.3 Hotel, airfare, and transportation expenses require supporting receipts regardless of amount.

7.4 Expense claims must be submitted within 15 calendar days after completion of the business trip.

7.5 Claims submitted after 30 calendar days require written justification and approval from the Finance Manager.

7.6 All expenses must be recorded in the original transaction currency, with the exchange rate and converted USD amount identified.

7.7 Finance may reject incomplete, duplicated, or unsupported claims.

8. CLIENT-HOSTED TRAVEL

8.1 Where a client or business partner provides accommodation, meals, or transportation, employees must disclose these arrangements in their travel request.

8.2 Employees must not claim reimbursement for expenses already paid by a client.

8.3 Any client-hosted travel arrangement that exceeds USD 500 in total value requires approval from the Department Head and Finance Manager.

9. GIFTS AND BUSINESS ENTERTAINMENT

9.1 Gifts and hospitality received or offered during business travel must comply with the Code of Conduct, Section 5.

9.2 Employees must disclose any gift, entertainment, or hospitality that could influence, or appear to influence, a business decision.

9.3 Company funds must not be used for personal entertainment or inappropriate hospitality.

10. COMPLIANCE AND REVIEW

10.1 Employees are responsible for the accuracy of their travel and expense claims.

10.2 Deliberate misrepresentation or fraudulent claims may result in disciplinary action.

10.3 Finance is responsible for reviewing expense submissions and maintaining financial records.

10.4 This policy will be reviewed annually.
