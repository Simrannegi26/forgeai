MARINER SHIPPING LTD.
SUPPLIER TERMS & CONDITIONS
Applicable to: Marine Spare Parts Suppliers
Document ID: PROC-POL-005
Version: 1.0

1. PURPOSE AND APPLICATION

1.1 This document establishes the commercial terms governing purchases of marine spare parts from Nordic Marine Supply, Pacific Ship Parts, and Anchor & Gear Co.

1.2 These terms apply to purchase orders issued by Mariner Shipping Ltd. procurement and authorised technical departments.

1.3 Each purchase order must identify the part number, description, quantity, unit price, delivery location, and required delivery date.

1.4 A supplier must acknowledge a purchase order within 2 working days. Any deviation from the agreed specifications must be communicated before order acceptance.

2. PRICING AND ORDER ACCEPTANCE

2.1 Prices must be quoted in United States Dollars (USD) unless an alternative currency is approved in writing.

2.2 Prices must remain valid for at least 30 calendar days from the quotation date.

2.3 Suppliers must not substitute components, manufacturers, or technical specifications without written approval from Mariner Shipping Ltd.

2.4 Any price adjustment exceeding 5% of the previously agreed quotation requires written approval from the Procurement Manager.

3. PAYMENT TERMS BY SUPPLIER

3.1 Nordic Marine Supply operates on Net 30 payment terms. Payment is due 30 calendar days after receipt of a valid invoice and confirmation of acceptable delivery.

3.2 Nordic Marine Supply offers a 2% early-payment discount where the invoice is paid within 10 calendar days.

3.3 Pacific Ship Parts operates on Net 45 payment terms. Payment is due 45 calendar days after receipt of a valid invoice and confirmation of acceptable delivery.

3.4 Pacific Ship Parts offers a 1.5% early-payment discount where the invoice is paid within 15 calendar days.

3.5 Anchor & Gear Co. operates on Net 30 payment terms. Payment is due 30 calendar days after receipt of a valid invoice and confirmation of acceptable delivery.

3.6 Anchor & Gear Co. offers a 3% early-payment discount where the invoice is paid within 7 calendar days.

3.7 Early-payment discounts apply only to undisputed invoices and must be clearly identified on the invoice or payment documentation.

3.8 Payments may be withheld for disputed quantities, damaged goods, missing documentation, or material specification differences until the issue is resolved.

4. MINIMUM ORDER VALUES

4.1 Nordic Marine Supply requires a minimum order value of USD 250.

4.2 Pacific Ship Parts requires a minimum order value of USD 200.

4.3 Anchor & Gear Co. requires a minimum order value of USD 500.

4.4 Orders below the applicable minimum may be accepted with written supplier confirmation and Procurement Manager approval.

4.5 The minimum order value is calculated before applicable taxes and delivery charges.

5. RETURNS AND WARRANTY

5.1 Nordic Marine Supply accepts return requests for unused and unopened standard spare parts within 30 calendar days of delivery.

5.2 Pacific Ship Parts accepts eligible return requests within 45 calendar days of delivery.

5.3 Anchor & Gear Co. accepts eligible return requests within 30 calendar days of delivery.

5.4 All three suppliers provide a standard 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects, commencing from the date of delivery.

5.5 Warranty claims must include the purchase order number, part number, delivery date, description of the defect, and available supporting evidence.

5.6 Suppliers must acknowledge a warranty claim within 5 working days and provide a proposed resolution within 10 working days.

5.7 Custom-manufactured, installed, modified, or improperly stored components may be excluded from standard returns, subject to the supplier's written terms and applicable law.

5.8 Return freight responsibility must be agreed in writing before a returned component is dispatched.

6. URGENT ORDER EXCEPTION

6.1 Urgent orders may be placed when a spare part is required to prevent significant vessel downtime or an operational interruption.

6.2 The Procurement Manager may authorise an urgent purchase outside the standard minimum order value.

6.3 For orders exceeding USD 5,000, approval from the Chief Operating Officer is also required.

6.4 Suppliers must confirm urgent-order availability and estimated dispatch timing before accepting the purchase order.

6.5 Any additional urgent-order charges must be disclosed in advance and approved in writing.

6.6 Urgent orders must still comply with technical specifications, documentation, and quality requirements.

7. DELIVERY OBLIGATIONS

7.1 Suppliers must meet the delivery windows and service commitments established in the Delivery SLA.

7.2 Nordic Marine Supply, Pacific Ship Parts, and Anchor & Gear Co. must provide shipment confirmation and tracking information after dispatch.

7.3 Suppliers must notify Mariner Shipping Ltd. within 1 working day of identifying a potential delivery delay.

7.4 Changes to agreed delivery dates require written approval from the Procurement Manager.

7.5 Late-delivery penalties, urgent-delivery surcharges, regional delivery commitments, and dispatch cut-off times are governed by the Delivery SLA, Sections 2 through 5.

8. QUALITY AND DOCUMENTATION

8.1 All spare parts must match the approved technical specifications and purchase order requirements.

8.2 Suppliers must provide relevant product identification, manufacturer details, and quality documentation where required.

8.3 Products must be packaged appropriately to prevent damage during normal transportation and handling.

8.4 Mariner Shipping Ltd. may reject parts that are damaged, incorrectly supplied, or materially inconsistent with the purchase order.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Commercial disputes should first be referred to the Procurement Manager for review.

9.2 The parties should attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith discussions within 15 working days.

9.3 Unresolved matters will be escalated to the respective senior commercial representatives.

9.4 No change to these terms will be effective unless documented and accepted by authorised representatives of both parties.

10. REVIEW AND ADMINISTRATION

10.1 The Procurement Department is responsible for maintaining supplier agreements and purchase records.

10.2 These terms will be reviewed annually or when significant commercial requirements change.

10.3 Any supplier-specific exception must be recorded against the relevant purchase order.

END OF DOCUMENT
